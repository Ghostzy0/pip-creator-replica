# Creator Baseline FAQ

### Is this the vidIQ app?
No. It is an independent offline baseline based on public documentation. It does not use vidIQ code, branding, private data or proprietary scores. No direct signed-in comparison has been performed.

### Does it connect to YouTube, estimate keyword demand or generate ideas?
No. It searches only your local imported titles and notes. It does not connect to accounts, call an AI provider, estimate search volume or calculate scores.

### Can I export selected records?
Yes. The app previews selected local records, then opens a native save panel for CSV or JSON. The app does not upload exports; the save destination is chosen by you and may be a synced folder.

### What is the difference from Pip Creator?
This repository is the Neutral Core documented-workflow baseline. The custom Neutral Plus, Pip Core and Pip Plus editions are in [pip-creator](https://github.com/Ghostzy0/pip-creator). Both use the same CreatorCore source design, but they ship as separate applications and data folders.

### Is the app ready for general distribution?
No. Local packages are ad-hoc signed and not notarized. The public repository offers source, not a verified binary release.
